🌤 What Overcast Is

Overcast is a desktop weather application for Windows. Its purpose is straightforward: to show you the weather in a way that is quick to read and easy to understand. The name comes from the idea of an overcast sky, and the tagline says the rest — weather is also essential.

Many weather tools try to do everything at once, layering radar maps, alerts, and long-range charts on top of each other. Overcast takes the opposite approach. It focuses on the information you actually act on: what the sky is doing now, what it will do next, and whether you should grab a jacket, an umbrella, or neither.
